# Sales Performance Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes retail sales performance using Power BI.  
The dashboard provides a complete view of sales trends, top-performing products, category performance, and regional sales distribution.

It is designed to support business decision-making by highlighting key revenue drivers, customer purchasing behavior, and sales patterns over time.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective
- Monitor overall sales performance
- Identify best-selling products and categories
- Analyze sales distribution across regions/states
- Track monthly sales trends and seasonality
- Support business decisions through interactive KPIs and filters

---

## 🛠 Tools Used
- **Power BI Desktop**
- **Power Query** (data cleaning and transformation)
- **DAX** (KPIs and measures)
- Dataset in CSV format

---

## 📂 Dataset Information
The dataset contains transactional sales data including:
- Order details (Order ID, Order Date, Ship Date, Ship Mode)
- Customer information (Customer ID, Customer Name, Segment)
- Geographic information (City, State, Region, Postal Code)
- Product details (Product ID, Product Name, Category, Sub-Category)
- Sales value (Sales)

---

## 🧹 Data Cleaning & Transformation
Data preparation steps applied:
- Fixed data types (dates, numeric values, text fields)
- Removed duplicates and handled missing values
- Created a calendar table for time-based analysis
- Created calculated columns and DAX measures for KPIs
- Standardized category and region fields

---

## 📊 Key KPIs Included
The dashboard includes the following KPIs:
- **Total Sales**
- **Average Sales per Order**
- **Total Orders**
- **Total Customers**
- **Sales Growth (MoM)**
- **Top Product Contribution**

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features
The report includes:
- KPI cards with executive summary metrics
- Monthly sales trend line chart
- Top 10 products by sales (bar chart)
- Sales by category (donut chart)
- Sales by region/state (map visualization)
- Interactive slicers for date, region, category, and segment

---

## 🔍 Key Insights (Examples)
- A small group of products generates a large percentage of total sales.
- Certain regions outperform others in revenue contribution.
- Sales show clear seasonality patterns across months.
- Some categories have consistent performance, while others fluctuate.
- Regional analysis helps identify areas with growth potential.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations
Based on the analysis:
- Focus marketing and promotions on high-performing regions.
- Prioritize inventory planning for top-selling products.
- Analyze underperforming categories to improve pricing or strategy.
- Use seasonal trends to plan campaigns and stock levels.
- Expand product offerings in regions with high demand.
